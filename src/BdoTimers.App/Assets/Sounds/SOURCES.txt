Built-in alert sounds

All eight are from Freesound, released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0, public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Credit is not required; it is given here with thanks. Each was cut to its first phrase (under two seconds), faded out,
levelled to match the others and saved as mono 16-bit WAV.
